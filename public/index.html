Confirm load rating

A Wi-Fi range extender can make an office feel more organized, but it should solve a real routine instead of adding another app to manage. The right choice depends on load rating, scheduling, voice control, network reliability, coverage zone fit, and whether staff can understand the automation without a long explanation.

Load rating is the first safety check. A Wi-Fi range extender that is fine for a access point may not be appropriate for heaters, large printers, coffee machines, or shared power strips.
